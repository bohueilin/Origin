## Shared patterns

`public/editorial.css` uses the shared home design tokens and scopes all layout rules to editorial or research page classes. Shared navigation and footer normalization belong to the root redesign. Legal pages gain shared typography without changing their legal substance. The research report keeps its self-contained data and diagrams.

Generated imagery is explicitly labeled as illustration. No image is presented as a facility, deployment, product recording, or customer proof.

## Contracts to preserve

- Public reference-check artifacts are synthetic, browser-session-signed, and unpinned; execution is not attempted.
- The deterministic oracle is the sole label/reward authority. No model-grades-model claim.
- Integrity checks do not establish trusted issuer identity, production readiness, or certification.
- Runtime enforcement and controlled tool-call proxies remain proposed pilot architecture.
- Authored specimens, machine-emitted sandbox traces, synthetic evaluation, generated counterfactuals, authorized fixtures, and real customer evidence remain separate.
- Research metrics use a pinned private pipeline and are not re-derivable from the public repository. Real customer readiness remains blocked.
- Existing artifact downloads, exact trace digest, proof anchors, canonical URLs, legal text, JSON-LD, and module mount points remain intact.
- Home stylesheet references must receive the final content hash after shared CSS is complete.
