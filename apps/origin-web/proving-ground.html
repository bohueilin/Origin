Draw the floor ↓

An Origin Labs experiment, not the product: the product is the agent reference check. This is a synthetic grid simulation with no physical robot connected. Geometry and robot types determine the result; playback is descriptive. It grants no real-world operating authority or safety certification. Signed records re-verify in the offline verifier.

How to read the result

What is scored. One lane on the floor you draw: the route from the start, which is not drawn on the grid, to the first item and then the first drop, on a grid of 4 to 12 cells a side with four-way moves.

Verdicts. Finish: a route avoids walls, hazards and human-only cells within the robot type's step and battery budgets. Refuse: the only routes that fit the budgets cross a hazard or human-only cell. Escalate: walls block every route, or none fits the budgets.

Robot types. The oracle scores each robot type on the floor once, in its own episode, including the type of a fleet with no robots. Types differ only by their step and battery budgets, so they usually share a verdict.

Limits. Where robots stand, the other items and drops, and the descriptive notes do not change the verdicts. No physics, sensing or real facility is represented.
