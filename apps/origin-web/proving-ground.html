One evidence spine · the robot proving ground

Draw the floor. Deploy the fleet. Earn the level.

Paint your own warehouse floor — walls, hazards, human-only zones — and deploy a mixed robot fleet: humanoid, AMR, quadruped, arm, drone. Watch the same floor work in 2D and 3D. Origin's deterministic oracle then scores one episode per robot type, and the fleet earns a Verified Readiness Level (L0–L4) sealed as a signed credential — the same evidence spine a digital agent action earns.

Robot placements are descriptive — they drive the animation, never the verdict. The oracle alone judges (never an LLM), a catastrophic episode hard-caps the level, and a level means "reproducible under this verifier" — never "safe." Synthetic floors; runs in your browser.
